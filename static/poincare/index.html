Conventions used here
  • |L⟩ = (1, i)/√2 is left circular and sits at the north pole: s₃ = (|E_L|² − |E_R|²)/S₀, exactly as in Aufgabe 8.2.
  • Wave plates at lab angle θ: M = R(θ)·diag(1, e^{iδ})·R(−θ), so λ/4 at 0° is diag(1, i) and λ/2 at 0° is diag(1, −1). The phase shifter is the birefringent crystal of Aufgabe 8.3 / the LC modulator of Aufgabe 9.2, with adjustable relative phase δ between the V and H components.
  • A lab angle θ appears at 2θ on the sphere (angle doubling). A retarder rotates the sphere by δ about its eigenstate axis (cos 2θ, sin 2θ, 0) in the equator plane — the dashed line shown during scans.
  • Polarizers project onto their transmission axis; the state vector is renormalized afterwards (only fully polarized states are shown) while the lost light is tracked in the intensity readout — Malus’s law lives there.
  • Scan = the chosen angle (or phase) runs 0° → 360° in 6 s; the trace shows the output of the whole bench, colored by the scanned value. Wave-plate and polarizer angles act with a 180° period, so the colormap repeats every half-turn — the two laps share the same colors instead of overwriting each other.
